> Documenting my participation and learning experience in a cybersecurity competition.

## Overview

**JCC Jatim Cyber Security Competition** was an online cybersecurity competition that challenged participants to explore technical problem-solving and cybersecurity concepts.

The competition brought together approximately 400 participating teams, with only the top 40 teams selected to advance to the next stage.

Although my team did not qualify for the next round, this competition provided a valuable opportunity to gain experience and explore the field of cybersecurity.
